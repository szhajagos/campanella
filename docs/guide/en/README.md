# Guide for site owners

This guide is for people who run a Campanella site: they upload and install
it, write content, manage the users and take care of the backups. No
programming knowledge is needed; where something is technical (editing a
settings file), it is described step by step.

The guide describes Campanella 0.4.x. The pictures show the English
interface, with Campanella's own sample content in English.

## Contents

1. [Installation](01-installation.md): uploading the files, the settings file,
   the installer in the browser.
2. [First steps](02-first-steps.md): logging in, the admin, the site's
   settings, the System page.
3. [Content](03-content.md): articles, pages, categories, the editor,
   publishing (later too), the search, redirects, deleting.
4. [Menus](04-menus.md): the main menu's items, drop-downs, the order.
5. [Images and files](05-images.md): uploading, alternative text, images in
   texts, files to download and who may download them, the media library,
   deleting.
6. [Users and your own account](06-users.md): roles, new users, blocking,
   the profile, logins, a forgotten password.
7. [The contact form and privacy](07-contact-form.md): the form, the
   messages, the privacy notice, the personal data kept.
8. [E-mail](08-e-mail.md): setting it up, trying it, the log, common problems.
9. [Backups and upgrades](09-backups-and-upgrades.md): what to back up and
   how, restoring, upgrading to a newer version.
10. [When something does not work](10-troubleshooting.md): common problems,
    their causes and what to do (the site's look too); where to get help.
11. [Your own kinds of content: an activity calendar](11-own-content-types.md):
    a new kind of content and its fields, the admin's list, a list for
    visitors with filters, carrying it to another site.
12. [Importing from a spreadsheet](12-importing.md): CSV and JSON files,
    setting up an importer, the trial, the log, undoing, the CSV export.
13. [Components](13-components.md): snippets, a component in three steps (the
    source, the display, the settings), who sees it, in a template.
14. [Layouts and regions](14-layouts.md): the header's and footer's
    components, a sidebar beside the articles, the regions' order, where a
    component shows, on small screens.
15. [Pages assembled from components](15-pages.md): a page's own components and
    layout (for editors too), a new component for a page, the front page.
16. [Colour schemes and the colour mode](16-colour-schemes.md): the site's colours
    in a light and a dark variant, the colour mode, the visitor's colour mode switch.
